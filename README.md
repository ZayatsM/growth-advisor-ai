 # AI Marketing Analyst

AI Marketing Analyst is a Python-based analytics project that helps businesses analyze customer and marketing data, identify high-value customer segments, evaluate campaign performance, and generate business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Small businesses often collect marketing and customer data but do not know how to turn it into clear decisions.

This project helps answer questions such as:

- Which customers are most valuable?
- Which campaigns perform best?
- What factors influence customer spending?
- What marketing actions should the business take next?

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Streamlit
- AI-assisted business reporting

## Key Features

- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Campaign Performance Analysis
- High-Value Customer Prediction
- AI-style Business Recommendations
- Interactive Streamlit Dashboard
