# Customer Intelligence Analysis

## Project Overview

Growth Advisor AI is a marketing analytics project designed to identify high-value customers, analyze purchasing behavior, and generate actionable business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Businesses collect large amounts of customer and marketing data but often struggle to convert that information into strategic decisions.

This analysis aims to answer:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customer characteristics drive spending?
- Which marketing channels are most effective?
- How can marketing performance be improved?

## Dataset

Customer Personality Analysis Dataset

Records: 2,240 customers

Features include:

- Income
- Education
- Marital Status
- Product Spending
- Purchase Channels
- Campaign Responses

## Analysis Plan

### 1. Data Overview

- Dataset dimensions
- Missing values
- Data types

### 2. Customer Demographics

- Income distribution
- Education levels
- Marital status

### 3. Spending Analysis

- Product category spending
- Total customer spending

### 4. High-Value Customer Identification

- Top spending segments
- Customer characteristics

### 5. Marketing Insights

- Campaign response analysis
- Purchase channel analysis

### 6. Business Recommendations

- Customer targeting
- Campaign optimization
- Growth opportunities
